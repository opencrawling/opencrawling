ACME Enterprise Handbook & Security Policy (Public Edition)

1. General Onboarding
Welcome to ACME Corporation. This handbook is classified as PUBLIC and is accessible to all employees, contractors, and guests across the organization.

2. Working Hours and Collaboration
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Teams use Alfresco Content Services for document management and OpenCrawling with Zero-Trust Model Context Protocol (MCP) search to retrieve approved corporate knowledge.

3. AI & Knowledge Retrieval Guidelines
All employees may query public company guidelines, office directories, and holiday schedules through AI assistants connected to OpenCrawling. Sensitive financial, architectural, and executive documents are enforced strictly by document-level Access Control Lists (ACLs).
